# Buyer Scoring Schema

## Purpose

This schema defines the output structure for scored buyer lead data generated by Task 007: Buyer Scoring Automation.

The scored output is for internal sales prioritization only. It must not claim that a buyer is real, financially reliable, creditworthy, or guaranteed to purchase. The automation must use only local buyer lead data that was manually provided or already exists in repository CSV files.

This schema does not implement scraping, live web research, automatic web search, browser automation, APIs, requests, crawlers, buyer enrichment, credit checks, or external data collection.

## Output Fields

The scored buyer lead output must include these fields exactly, in this order:

1. `buyer_id`
2. `score_total`
3. `priority_tier`
4. `scoring_summary`
5. `positive_factors`
6. `negative_factors`
7. `risk_flags`
8. `approval_block`
9. `recommended_next_action`
10. `scoring_version`
11. `scored_at`

## Field Definitions

| Field | Meaning | Required | Format | Allowed values | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| `buyer_id` | Stable buyer lead identifier from the input buyer master CSV. | Required | Text ID matching input, preferably `BUYER-0001` style. | Must match a `buyer_id` from the input buyer master CSV. | `BUYER-0001` | Must not be invented, changed, or normalized differently from the input. |
| `score_total` | Internal sales-priority score. | Required | Numeric, recommended integer. | Recommended range: `0` to `100`. | `82` | This is not buyer authenticity, creditworthiness, financial reliability, or purchase probability. |
| `priority_tier` | Internal follow-up priority tier. | Required | Controlled text value. | `A`, `B`, `C`, `Hold` | `A` | Tier must follow the priority tier guidance below. |
| `scoring_summary` | Korean text summary explaining why the score and tier were assigned. | Required | UTF-8 Korean text. | Free text. | `MOQ는 충족하지만 결제 리스크가 미확인이라 내부 확인이 필요합니다.` | Must include limitations when data is missing, manually estimated, or insufficient. |
| `positive_factors` | Factors that increased the internal priority score. | Required | Semicolon-separated list or UTF-8 text. | Free text based only on input fields. | `MOQ 충족; 중국 관련성 높음; 반복구매 가능성 높음` | Must not include externally inferred facts. |
| `negative_factors` | Factors that reduced the internal priority score. | Required | Semicolon-separated list or UTF-8 text. | Free text based only on input fields. | `결제 리스크 높음; 수량 미확인; buyer_type unknown` | Must include key weaknesses or missing data that affected score. |
| `risk_flags` | Machine-readable or semi-structured caution flags. | Required | Semicolon-separated list or UTF-8 text. | Recommended values listed in the Risk Flag Guidance section. | `moq_unknown; buyer_unverified; source_manual_only` | Must include relevant MOQ, payment, approval, privacy, or evidence limitations. |
| `approval_block` | Whether brand approval rules block external proposal readiness. | Required | Boolean text. | `true`, `false` | `true` | Must be `true` when approval warning exists, `proposal_brand_check=approval_required_review`, or `interested_brands` includes `메디큐브` without explicit approval. |
| `recommended_next_action` | Practical internal sales follow-up guidance. | Required | UTF-8 Korean text. | Free text. | `수량을 확인한 뒤 내부 승인 검토 후 제안 가능 브랜드만 정리합니다.` | Must not recommend external proposal, buyer-facing pitch, quotation, public content, or ad copy for approval-blocked brands. |
| `scoring_version` | Version of scoring rules used. | Required | Text version identifier. | Recommended pattern: `buyer_scoring_v1.0` | `buyer_scoring_v1.0` | Must change when scoring logic or weight rules change materially. |
| `scored_at` | Date when scoring was generated. | Required | ISO date. | `YYYY-MM-DD` | `2026-06-08` | Use the local generation date. |

## Priority Tier Guidance

| Tier | Meaning | Typical Conditions | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A` | High internal follow-up priority. | Strong fit, `moq_fit=yes`, low or medium payment risk, useful buyer type/channel, clear next action, and no blocking approval issue. | Must still be reviewed manually before external proposal. |
| `B` | Medium internal follow-up priority. | Reasonable fit, some missing information, moderate risk, or follow-up needed. | Suitable for internal follow-up after clarifying weak points. |
| `C` | Low internal follow-up priority. | Low fit, `moq_fit=no`, weak buyer/channel fit, or low immediate readiness. | May improve if quantity, buyer type, or next action changes. |
| `Hold` | Do not prioritize until key issue is resolved. | Approval block, high risk, missing critical information, no actionable follow-up, or severe data-quality issue. | Hold is not rejection; it means internal review is required first. |

The priority tier is an internal workflow label only. It must not be described as proof of buyer authenticity, verified payment reliability, or purchase probability.

## Risk Flag Guidance

Recommended `risk_flags` values include:

| Risk flag | Meaning | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| `moq_not_met` | Buyer appears below the usual MOQ of 100+ units. | Use when `moq_fit=no`. |
| `moq_unknown` | MOQ fit cannot be confirmed. | Use when `moq_fit=unknown` or estimated quantity is blank, 0, unknown, or non-numeric. |
| `payment_risk_high` | Payment risk is manually marked high. | Use when `payment_risk=high`. |
| `payment_risk_unknown` | Payment risk has not been assessed. | Use when `payment_risk=unknown` or blank. |
| `approval_required_brand` | Buyer interest includes an approval-required or proposal-blocked brand. | Use when `approval_warning` exists, `proposal_brand_check=approval_required_review`, or interested brands include `메디큐브`. |
| `contact_incomplete` | Follow-up contact details are missing or incomplete. | Use when practical follow-up cannot proceed due to missing contact fields. |
| `privacy_review_needed` | Contact or buyer data requires privacy review before sharing. | Use for real buyer data or outputs intended beyond internal use. |
| `buyer_unverified` | Buyer authenticity has not been verified by this automation. | Use by default when the scoring output might be mistaken for verification. |
| `source_manual_only` | Data is based only on manually provided local records. | Use by default for Task 007 outputs. |
| `critical_data_missing` | Critical fields are missing. | Use when missing data prevents meaningful scoring or follow-up. |

Additional flags may be added later, but they must remain based on local input data and must not imply external verification.

## Approval Block Rules

`approval_block` must be `true` when any of the following are true:

- `approval_warning` exists in the input buyer master row.
- `proposal_brand_check=approval_required_review`.
- `interested_brands` includes `메디큐브` and explicit approval is not recorded.
- A future local brand approval reference indicates `approval_required=true`.
- A future local brand approval reference indicates `proposal_allowed=false`.

When `approval_block=true`:

- External proposal must not proceed for the approval-required brand.
- Buyer-facing pitch must not proceed for the approval-required brand.
- Quotation must not proceed for the approval-required brand.
- Public content must not proceed for the approval-required brand.
- Advertising copy must not proceed for the approval-required brand.
- `recommended_next_action` must direct the team to internal approval review first.

A high `score_total` or `priority_tier=A` must not override approval restrictions.

`메디큐브` remains approval-required unless explicit approval is provided.

## MOQ Rules

MOQ is generally 100+ units.

| Input condition | Required scoring interpretation |
|---|---|
| `estimated_order_qty >= 100` and `moq_fit=yes` | Treat as MOQ suitable and add a positive factor. |
| `estimated_order_qty` between `1` and `99` and `moq_fit=no` | Treat as MOQ not met, reduce score, and add `moq_not_met`. |
| Blank, unknown, `0`, or non-numeric `estimated_order_qty` and `moq_fit=unknown` | Treat as unconfirmed, add `moq_unknown`, and recommend quantity confirmation. |
| Quantity and `moq_fit` are inconsistent | Treat as data-quality risk and require review before relying on the score. |

The scored output must not silently correct the source buyer master CSV.

## Business Rules

- Buyer scoring is for internal sales prioritization only.
- Scoring must not claim that a buyer is real, financially reliable, creditworthy, or guaranteed to purchase.
- Payment risk is a manual indicator only, not credit verification.
- Buyer authenticity is not verified by this automation.
- Contact and privacy-sensitive fields should not be copied into scoring output unless necessary.
- Approval-required brands must not become externally proposal-ready because of a high score.
- `메디큐브` must remain approval-required unless explicit approval is provided.
- If evidence is insufficient, `scoring_summary` and `risk_flags` must clearly state the limitation.
- Korean, English, and Chinese text must be preserved.
- CSV outputs intended for Excel or business users must use UTF-8 with BOM, `utf-8-sig`.

## Validation Requirements

The scored buyer output must be validated for:

- Required scoring columns exist in exact order:
  - `buyer_id`
  - `score_total`
  - `priority_tier`
  - `scoring_summary`
  - `positive_factors`
  - `negative_factors`
  - `risk_flags`
  - `approval_block`
  - `recommended_next_action`
  - `scoring_version`
  - `scored_at`
- Row count matches the input buyer master CSV.
- `buyer_id` values match the input buyer master CSV.
- `buyer_id` values are unique.
- `score_total` is numeric and between `0` and `100`.
- `priority_tier` is one of `A`, `B`, `C`, or `Hold`.
- `approval_block` is one of `true` or `false`.
- `approval_block=true` when `proposal_brand_check=approval_required_review`.
- `approval_block=true` when `interested_brands` includes `메디큐브` without explicit approval.
- `risk_flags` exist for `moq_fit=no`.
- `risk_flags` exist for `moq_fit=unknown`.
- `recommended_next_action` is not blank.
- `scoring_summary` is not blank.
- Korean and Chinese text is preserved.
- No real personal data is introduced.
- No external data collection is implied.
- Approval-required brands are not recommended externally.
